Frequently asked questions

What is a credit?
One credit = one AI image generation. Each time a customer tries on a hairstyle, it uses one credit.
What happens when I run out of credits?
The tool pauses until your credits renew next month or you upgrade your plan. Your customers see a polite message asking them to speak to you.
Do unused credits roll over?
Yes. Every month your new credits are added to whatever you have remaining. They never reset to zero.
Can my customers use it on their phones?
Yes. It works on any device with a browser — phone, tablet, or iPad. No app download required.
What if I want to cancel?
Cancel anytime from your account page. Your account stays active until the end of your billing period then closes automatically. No questions asked.
How do my customers use it?
You open the tool on your phone, tablet or iPad in your shop. Customers use it before their cut — they choose their style and see the AI result. You see exactly what they want before touching their hair.
