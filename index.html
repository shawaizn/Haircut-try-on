Questions

What is a credit?
One credit = one AI image generation. Each time a customer tries on a hairstyle, it uses one credit from your balance.
Do unused credits roll over?
Yes. Every month your new credits are added on top of whatever you have left. They never reset to zero as long as your subscription is active.
What happens when I run out of credits?
The tool pauses until your credits renew next month, or you upgrade your plan from the dashboard. Customers see a polite message asking them to speak to you directly.
Does my customer need to download anything?
No. It runs entirely in the browser — on any phone, tablet, or iPad. You open the tool, hand them the device, and they're off. No accounts, no downloads, no friction.
What does the Trial tier actually include?
The £1 Trial gives you 20 AI try-ons and full access to your barber dashboard for one month. It does not automatically upgrade or renew — after one month the account closes and you decide if you'd like to continue on a paid plan.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes. Cancel from your account dashboard at any time. Your access continues until the end of the billing period, then closes automatically. No questions asked.
